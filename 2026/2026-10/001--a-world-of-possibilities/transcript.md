### [04:00 – 08:30] The Four-Step DAWN Product Vertical

**[04:00]** At Prometheus, we organize our solutioning into a coherent four-step product vertical: **Marketing, Federation, Security, and CRM/ERP**. These are not isolated service labels; they represent the natural progression of building a sustainable digital footprint.

#### Step 1: Marketing — Owned Publishing & Mission Discovery [04:15]
Marketing begins with clarity. An organization must articulate who it is, what it offers, whom it serves, and what action a visitor should take. 
- We move you away from third-party surveillance and algorithmic dependency onto **owned publishing**. 
- Your website becomes the canonical home for your articles, resources, and offers. 
- Using file-based publishing, Markdown, HTML, PHP, and structured article metadata, we construct clean, lightning-fast landing pages with transparent calls to action—whether to inquire, join, book, or buy.

#### Step 2: Federation — Connecting Independent Homes [05:15]
Federation is about network strength without central control. 
- We connect independent digital nodes—organizations, partners, and community members—without absorbing them into a monolithic central portal or shared database.
- Using open web standards, structured human-readable links, and clear consent boundaries, each member retains total local governance over their data while participating in a shared network directory and referral ecosystem. 
- You gain the benefit of a connected network while retaining complete exit assurance.

#### Step 3: Security — Responsibility by Design [06:30]
Security is not a decorative feature bolted on at the end—it is built into our core architecture.
- **Zero SQL & Zero Login Bloat:** For ordinary public publishing, we eliminate database logins and dynamic query vulnerabilities entirely.
- **Git Version Control & Static Files:** Content is stored as clean files and backed up via Git. 
- **Offline-First Safety:** Content is created and reviewed locally before deploying via clean FTP uploads. 
- **Structured Email Workflows:** Instead of vulnerable form-processing scripts and annoying CAPTCHAs, visitors open structured draft emails directly in their own mail clients, keeping data exchange transparent and consent-driven.

#### Step 4: CRM / ERP — Connecting Relationships to Delivery [07:30]
Where public engagement meets internal operations, our CRM/ERP layer connects the dots.
- Public inquiries flow into consented, private contact records (CRM).
- Accepted engagements transition directly into operational project registers, task tracking, and service delivery workflows (ERP).
- We maintain a strict boundary between public marketing content and private operational data, keeping records scoped strictly to the collecting organization.

### [08:30 – 11:30] The Technology Engine: AmadeusWeb Spring v9.9

**[08:30]** Powering this entire ecosystem is **AmadeusWeb Spring v9.9**, a mature, 7-year-old proprietary framework built on open web standards. It is the culmination of my 25 years of framework-building experience.

**[09:15]** Here is how AmadeusWeb Spring transforms site building:
1. **Folder-Hierarchy Navigation:** Your web structure matches your local directory hierarchy (`Section -> Node -> Subnode -> Page`). Navigation menus are generated automatically by scanning folders and files.
2. **Multi-Format Content Support:** Mix Markdown, HTML, raw PHP, TSV spreadsheets, Reveal.js presentation decks, and raw AI chat exports ("WithAI") seamlessly within the same folder structure.
3. **Decoupled CDN Assets:** Media assets (images, PDFs) can be offloaded to CDN mapping variables, preventing repository bloat while keeping public sites lightweight.
4. **Offline-First Development:** Developers and non-technical staff edit files locally using **VS Code** and track history using **TortoiseGit**. Work is reviewed on a local machine before being published.
