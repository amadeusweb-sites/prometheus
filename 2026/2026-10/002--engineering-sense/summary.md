## 3. The 4-Step DAWN Product Vertical

Prometheus translates organizational goals into self-reliant web platforms through a structured four-stage product vertical:

1. **Step 1: Marketing (Owned Publishing & Mission Discovery)**  
   Establishing clear mission positioning, clean offer branding, and owned web properties that attract genuine community alignment rather than algorithmic dependency.
2. **Step 2: Federation (Decentralized Node Interoperability)**  
   Connecting independent digital homes across open web protocols. Nodes share content, events, and catalogs while maintaining complete local governance and data ownership.
3. **Step 4: Security (Data Minimisation & Risk Reduction)**  
   Eliminating database attack vectors, SQL injection risks, and login vulnerabilities through static file-based publishing, Git version backups, and consent-based `mailto:` interaction loops.
4. **Step 4: CRM & ERP (Connected Operational Workflows)**  
   Bridging public inquiries directly into structured, permissioned CRM records and operational project registers—ensuring leads turn into delivered services without intermediate data leaks.

## 4. Technology Engine: AmadeusWeb Spring v9.9

At the core of every Prometheus deployment is **AmadeusWeb Spring v9.9**, an ultralight, file-based content management system and static site generator designed for speed, stability, and longevity:

* **Folder-Derived Navigation:** Automatically maps directories (`Section -> Node -> Subnode -> Page`) into human-readable URLs and clean site menus.
* **Zero Database Overhead:** No SQL databases to patch, compromise, or maintain—reducing server hosting costs and eliminating zero-day login exploits.
* **Offline-First Workflow:** Full editing support in local tools like VS Code, versioning via Git/TortoiseGit, and rapid deployment over FTP/SFTP.
* **Multi-Format Versatility:** Native support for Markdown, HTML, PHP, TSV spreadsheet tables, Reveal.js slide decks, and structured AI chat export logs.
