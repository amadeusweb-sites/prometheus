## Problem Statement

Care homes, shelter homes, government hospitals and Indian AYUSH facilities need dependable records that follow a person’s care and open actions across teams, shifts and referrals. A record is useful only when the right person can find it at the right time, understand its status and act on it. Where appropriate and authorized, the relevant information or a secure notification must reach the responsible team and the patient or their authorized carer without relying on informal forwarding or a public notice.

The [care homes overview](../) describes the continuity, handover and privacy problems. This proposal introduces the [Teams proposal](%urlOf-root%audiences/teams/) as an operating model: structured requests, named owners, shared status, email or message templates, and links to the authoritative record rather than uncontrolled copies. AmadeusWeb Spring can be the public-facing front door for approved information and process guidance. It is not a patient-record system, identity-management service or secure records-delivery channel.

“Directly to the concerned team / patient / patient carer” means routing a task or notification to an explicitly identified, authorized recipient and providing access to relevant records only through a channel approved by the facility. It does not mean publishing personal details, sending a full case file to a mailing list, or assuming that a family member is authorized to receive information. Verify identity, authority, consent or other valid basis, destination and minimum necessary disclosure before sharing. Clinical decisions and records remain under the responsible professionals and the facility’s applicable policies and legal requirements.

The migration has four starting situations. Use the stage that best describes the facility now; move forward only when the foundations of the next stage are in place.

## Bringing It All Home

The migration path is process first, then carefully chosen tools: establish roles and a minimum safe workflow, stabilize existing records, pilot one route, and expand only after evidence that it works. Teams can use structured templates, named owners, status changes and follow-ups to make responsibility visible. Patients and authorized carers should receive timely, appropriate communication, and the concerned team should receive the task or clinical notification it is responsible for.

An integrated public website is a public information and process layer—not a public window into private records. Use AmadeusWeb Spring for approved service information, forms, policies and de-identified learning. Keep identifiable records in the facility’s authorized system and deliver them only through an approved, access-controlled channel after verifying recipient identity and authority. Have facility leadership, clinical leads, records/privacy owners and the people doing the work approve each stage before expanding it.
